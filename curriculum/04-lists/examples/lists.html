Jollof rice (simplified)

Ingredients

  • 3 cups long-grain parboiled rice
  • Tomato and pepper base
    • 4 plum tomatoes
    • 2 red bell peppers
    • 1 scotch bonnet
  • Stock, onions, thyme, bay leaves, salt

Method

  1. Blend the tomato and pepper base.
  2. Fry onions, then the base, until the oil separates.
  3. Add stock and seasoning; bring to a boil.
  4. Add washed rice, cover, and cook on low heat.

Continue numbering after a note

Once the rice is soft:

  1. Stir gently and turn off the heat.
